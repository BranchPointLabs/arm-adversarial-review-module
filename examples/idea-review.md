ARM REVIEW

Verdict: Needs Validation
Confidence: Medium

Top Findings:

3. [MEDIUM] PM: Success metrics are vague. Impact cannot be judged. Define measurable adoption and time-saved targets.
4. [MEDIUM] Customer: Workflow fit is unproven. Users may ignore generated actions. Test with real transcripts and CRM users.

Risks:
1. Low adoption. Mitigate with prototype testing.
2. CRM data quality issues. Mitigate with human approval before sync.

Assumptions:
1. Sales reps trust generated action items. Validate with user test.
2. CRM updates are the real bottleneck. Validate with workflow study.

Open Questions:
1. Which sales role has the highest pain from call follow-up?
2. What CRM fields must be updated for this to matter?

Next Step:
1. Run a one-week prototype test with 5 sales reps before approving build.
