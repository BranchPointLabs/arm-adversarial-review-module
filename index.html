Local-first product review

Keep one working document sharp.

ARM helps technical PMs and engineering leads review an idea or PRD without turning it into a long AI essay. It produces small cards, lets you decide what matters, and updates the active document.

BMAD vs. ARM

Different jobs, different tools.

BMAD is a broader methodology. ARM is a narrower review surface for keeping the current document useful.

Workflow

The loop is intentionally small.

  1. Write or open a document. The selected document is the context for the review.
  2. Ask for review. Use chat, product, technical, or combined review.
  3. Work through cards. Keep useful questions, warnings, and next steps.
  4. Update the document. Accepted notes and decisions improve the source of truth.

Good fit

Use ARM when decisions need pressure.

  • Early product ideas that need sharper framing.
  • PRDs that need product and engineering review.
  • Technical direction that needs risks surfaced before build.
  • Projects where the context should stay local.
