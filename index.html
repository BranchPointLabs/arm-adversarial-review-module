ARM

Adversarial review for product documents.

Ask targeted questions about your PRDs and ideas. ARM returns structured, focused feedback so you can identify risks, evaluate decisions, and evolve the document without handing it over to AI.

The problem

Most AI tools generate documents.

That creates new work instead of removing it.

  • You have to verify everything.
  • Subtle inaccuracies slip in.
  • Your original intent gets lost.
  • Maintaining the document becomes harder over time.

The ARM approach

ARM does not generate documents.

ARM works with your document. You stay in control of the artifact.

You

  • Ask specific questions.
  • Focus on areas that matter.
  • Decide what changes.

ARM

  • Returns targeted feedback.
  • Highlights risks and gaps.
  • Keeps responses concise and usable.

No walls of text. No full rewrites.

How it works

Ask. Review. Decide. Evolve.

  1. Ask Ask focused questions like “What risks am I missing?” or “Where could this fail?”
  2. Review ARM returns scoped feedback that is easy to scan and evaluate.
  3. Decide Accept, ignore, or capture feedback as decisions while preserving your intent.
  4. Evolve Apply targeted changes without wholesale rewrites.

Core principles

Built for controlled document evolution.

User-directed

You control what gets reviewed and how.

Targeted feedback

No generic output. Only focused responses to your queries.

Structured output

Designed to be consumed quickly, not read like an essay.

Controlled evolution

Documents improve incrementally, not through wholesale replacement.

Most tools generate more content. ARM helps you think better about the content you already have.

AI does not replace your document. It challenges it.
