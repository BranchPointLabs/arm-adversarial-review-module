Why ARM vs BMAD Use

ARM

Adversarial review. Not document generation.

ARM reviews product ideas, PRDs, RFCs, and plans by returning small critique cards you can accept, reject, or edit.

It is built as a direct counterpoint to BMAD-style document generation: less output, more control.

Why

Generated documents are fast. Verifying them is slow.

Large AI-generated documents are hard to audit. Key flows can be missed, wording changes can alter meaning, and later generations can quietly add requirements.

ARM keeps the review surface small. It pressures the document without replacing it.

ARM vs BMAD

Output vs control.

BMAD

  • Generates large artifacts.
  • Requires comprehensive documentation.
  • Optimizes for speed and volume.
  • Leaves evaluation to the user.

ARM

  • Returns focused critique cards.
  • Works against the document you already have.
  • Optimizes for direction and correctness.
  • Captures decisions, risks, notes, and open questions.

Use

Ask. Review. Decide.

Ask a targeted question. ARM returns cards for risks, contradictions, questions, actions, and decision candidates. You decide what survives.

BMAD helps produce documents. ARM helps keep product decisions controlled and reviewable.
