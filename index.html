ARM

Adversarial review. Not document generation.

Most AI tools write more product documents. ARM does the opposite: it challenges the document you already have.

Ask a specific question. Get focused critique. Decide what changes.

Direct contrast

ARM exists because BMAD solves the wrong problem.

BMAD

  • Simulates roles.
  • Generates artifacts.
  • Produces long documents.
  • Leaves you to verify the output.

ARM

  • Applies pressure.
  • Returns review cards.
  • Forces decisions.
  • Keeps the document under your control.

BMAD helps you write. ARM helps you think.

The problem

More text usually means less clarity.

Large AI-generated documents feel productive, but they create a review burden. You still have to find the weak assumptions, hidden contradictions, and vague commitments.

ARM is built for the moment before engineering handoff, when the question is not “Can AI write this?” but “Should we build this, and what exactly are we committing to?”

How it works

Ask. Review. Decide. Update.

  1. Ask Ask a targeted question about a PRD, idea, RFC, or plan.
  2. Review ARM returns concise critique cards instead of essays.
  3. Decide Accept, reject, or edit the feedback.
  4. Update Use only the accepted feedback to evolve the document.

Principles

Decisions over documents.

  • Pressure over generation.
  • Cards over walls of text.
  • User control over autonomous rewrite.

Most tools generate more content. ARM makes your existing content survive scrutiny.
