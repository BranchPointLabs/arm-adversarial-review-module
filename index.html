Sharper decisions, less document sprawl

ARM turns rough thinking into review cards and cleaner project truth.

ARM is a local-first desktop tool for technical PMs and product teams. It keeps the working document in the center, then layers notes, decisions, references, and adversarial review around it.

Core loop

  1. Capture a note, prompt, or draft.
  2. Run product or engineering review.
  3. Accept, reject, or edit the review cards.
  4. Rewrite the active document with the useful signal.

What ARM is

ARM is not a giant workflow system. It is a focused review surface for pressure-testing ideas, PRDs, and technical direction before they get bloated or quietly drift.

Designed for

  • technical PMs
  • product leads
  • founders shaping product direction
  • engineering leads reviewing scope and risk

BMAD vs. ARM

BMAD is broad and process-heavy. ARM is narrower: review the current document, expose the important gaps, and help the user update the source of truth.
